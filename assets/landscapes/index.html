Lennard-Jones particle permeation through graphene nanowindows

A nanowindow is a hole made by removing n carbon atoms (8 to 32) from a rigid graphene sheet. The surface shows the potential energy of a single argon atom over the sheet: at each lateral position (x, y) the atom's height is relaxed to its lowest-energy value. The black line is the permeation path from an adsorption site on the basal plane into the nanowindow, and its highest point is the transition state that sets the activation barrier.
