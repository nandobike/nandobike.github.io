The specimen is a film cut from the periodic structure, perpendicular to the chosen cell axis, and laterally continuous. The double-tilt holder turns it in the fixed vertical beam, so the beam's path through the film grows as 1/cos(tilt). The simulated window is 1.2 nm wider than the field on every side, so tilted films have no wrap-around seams. At zero tilt, with the beam along c and one cell of thickness, the image matches the gallery's HRTEM view, which in turn correlates at r ≈ 0.93 with the paper's MacTempas images.

Physics: Kirkland's carbon scattering factors, phase grating and Fresnel propagation with a 2/3 band limit, and Debye–Waller damping (B = 0.5 Å²). Focal spread is averaged over 7 Gauss–Hermite foci, and beam divergence is a quasi-coherent envelope. Noise is Poisson at the set dose, seen through a detector with a 0.8 Å⁻¹ Gaussian MTF.
